常見問題 FAQ

點樣玩？ / How do I play?

你同 3 個電腦玩家各有 13 張牌。持階磚 3 嘅人先出，之後每人要出同樣張數、而且更大嘅牌，或者「唔要」。最快出晒牌嘅人贏。撳「玩法」睇完整規則。
You and three computer players get 13 cards each. Whoever holds the 3 of diamonds starts. Each player must play the same number of cards that beats the table, or pass. First to play all cards wins. Tap Rules in the app for details.

教練點運作？ / How does the Coach work?

開啟教練後，輪到你時會提示建議出咩牌；揀牌時話你知合唔合法；出完牌會評分。撳「提示」會自動揀好建議嘅牌。
With the Coach on, you get a suggested play on your turn, live feedback while selecting cards, and a rating after each play. Hint selects the suggested cards for you.

分數有咩用？ / What are points for?

分數只係記錄你嘅成績同等級，唔可以購買、兌換或者用嚟換任何嘢。本 App 冇廣告、冇內購、冇金錢。
Points only track your results and level. They cannot be bought, exchanged or used for anything. No ads, no in-app purchases, no money.

旁述語音點開？ / How do I turn on the voice narrator?

撳右上角選單 ≡ → 設定 → 旁述語音，揀男聲或女聲。
Tap the ≡ menu → Settings → Voice narrator, then choose Male or Female.

冇聲？ / No sound?

音效會跟隨 iPhone 嘅靜音鍵。請關閉靜音，並喺選單設定確認音效已開啟。
Sound follows your silent switch. Turn off silent mode and check that Sound is on in the menu settings.
