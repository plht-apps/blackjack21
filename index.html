常見問題 FAQ

籌碼用完怎麼辦？ / I ran out of chips.

籌碼用完時遊戲會免費再送你 1,000 籌碼。每天登入亦可領取每日獎勵，連續 7 天最高 2,000。
When you run out, the game gives you 1,000 free chips. You also get a daily login bonus — up to 2,000 on day 7.

可以用真錢嗎？ / Can I play for real money?

不可以。本遊戲只使用虛擬籌碼，僅供娛樂及學習策略，不能購買、兌換或提取。
No. The game uses virtual chips only, for entertainment and strategy practice. They cannot be bought, exchanged or cashed out.

教練模式怎樣運作？ / How does the Coach work?

教練根據 21 點「基本策略」（6 副牌、莊家 17 點停牌），即時把最佳玩法的按鈕亮起金框，並記錄你的準確度。可在畫面上方開關。
The Coach follows standard blackjack basic strategy (6 decks, dealer stands on 17), highlights the best move in gold and tracks your accuracy. Toggle it at the top of the screen.

每日挑戰是甚麼？ / What is the Daily Challenge?

每天一次，全球玩家使用同一副洗好的牌，從 1,000 籌碼開始玩 10 局（每局最多下注 200），以最後籌碼數登上 Game Center 排行榜。
Once a day, everyone plays the same shuffled shoe: 10 hands starting from 1,000 chips (max bet 200 per hand). Your final chips go to the Game Center leaderboard.

排行榜看不到我的成績？ / My score isn’t on the leaderboard.

請確認 iPhone「設定 > Game Center」已登入，並在完成每日挑戰時保持網絡連線。
Make sure you’re signed in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center) and online when you finish the Daily Challenge.

怎樣解鎖新牌桌和牌背？ / How do I unlock new tables and card backs?

每局都會獲得經驗值，升級就會解鎖新主題（Lv.2、3、4、5、6、7、8、10）。在選單中選用。
You earn XP every hand. Levelling up unlocks new themes (Lv. 2–10). Choose them in the menu.

沒有聲音？ / No sound?

音效會跟隨 iPhone 的靜音鍵。請關閉靜音，並在選單「設定」確認「音效」已開啟。
Sound follows your silent switch. Turn off silent mode and check that Sound is on in the menu settings.

換新手機後進度會保留嗎？ / Will my progress transfer to a new phone?

遊戲進度儲存在裝置本機，透過 iCloud 或電腦備份還原 iPhone 時會一併還原。
Progress is stored on your device and is restored when you restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup.
